TowerTaq Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: TowerTaq does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. The game works entirely on your device.

This policy explains how the iPhone game TowerTaq ("the app", "we") handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or any other personal information.

Information stored on your device

To work as you expect, the app keeps a few small settings on your device only: your best score and your choices for the theme, music and sound. This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete the app.

Network and third parties

The app does not connect to the internet and does not include third-party advertising, analytics or social-media services. We do not share any information with anyone, because we do not have any.

TestFlight

If you install a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect information such as crash reports and the feedback you choose to send, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We receive that feedback only through TestFlight and use it only to fix problems with the app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online features, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue in this project's GitHub repository.
